The Hopeful Humanist
— Tools

Three free tools for thinking more clearly about choice, wellbeing, and power.

Each tool in this suite was built from personal experience — not from theory alone. They are offered freely, without sign-up, without data collection, and without agenda beyond the one stated in each tool itself.

Tool One

The C³hoice Lab

May the choices you make be the wisest ones possible in the moments you meet them.

A decision-making tool built on thirty years of thinking about how human beings actually choose. Work through real decisions step by step — naming your situation, mapping your options, identifying what matters most, and checking alignment with who you actually are. Includes a voice mentor, daily prompts, a choosing leaning questionnaire, and a printable workbook.

Free · No sign-up · Works on any phone

Tool Two

AI Wellness

Human connection is always the goal. The protocol holds even when it's hard.

Ten protocols to help young people protect their mental and emotional health before and after AI interactions. A free, non-commercial youth mental health literacy tool — not a therapeutic AI product, but a practical guide to thinking critically about AI use. Built from personal phenomenological experience. Includes before/after checklists, reflection prompts, three conditions worth being aware of, a case study, and two workshop worksheets.

Free · Creative Commons BY-NC 4.0

Launch

Tool Three

Pawn, Serf, Sheep

The powerful don't play. The rest of us do.

A satirical decision-making tool and hand gesture game for people who don't get to choose their battles. Built on rock-paper-scissors logic with a techno-feudal framing. Pawn beats Serf. Serf beats Sheep. Sheep beats Pawn. Nobody beats the Overlord. The Overlord is not a variable. Includes a two-player app, a workplace classification survey, a field guide, and the complete record of the first AI vs AI tournament ever played with the game.

Free · Non-commercial · May 2026

The Hopeful Humanist

The C³ framework has been developing since 1991 — rooted in a decision flow chart and a conviction that human beings are, at their core, choosing creatures. The tools in this suite grew from that conviction. Each one was built from personal experience, offered freely, and designed to help people think more clearly about choice, wellbeing, and power. Steve explores these ideas further on The Hopeful Humanist Café podcast.
